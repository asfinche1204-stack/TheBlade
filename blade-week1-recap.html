Bidding in a guillotine
Three things this desk believes
  • The pool gets better every week — every roster that drops was built by somebody who lasted longer than the last one — but the number of weeks you can use what you buy shrinks every week too. An early lot buys the most Sundays.
  • $200 is the whole season. There are sixteen more estate sales. Spending $80 on a Week 1 lot means watching a Week 9 roster with two stars on it go to somebody with money left.
  • Bid for the slot you're actually starting, not the name. The blade only reads your starting nine. Bench depth is for leagues with byes to worry about; here it's for the week your starter gets hurt on Thursday.
